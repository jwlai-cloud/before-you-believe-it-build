Before You Believe It

A five-to-seven-minute family activity that turns a confident AI answer into a shared practice of pausing, questioning, checking, and thinking together.

01

Think

The child chooses the question that makes a plausible claim worth pausing over.

02

Push Back

The family reveals the hidden assumption doing work inside the claim.

03

Check

They connect a clue or counter-perspective—not a verdict—to the claim.

04

Make

The child assembles a provisional answer in their own words.

05

Own

A Learning Receipt separates child work, AI help, evidence, uncertainty, and a parent question.

Static first.
The complete Floating City MissionPack works without a key or network.
AI is bounded.
GPT-5.6 prepares material to question; it never grades, ranks, diagnoses, or decides.
Child work stays local.
The live request receives only a general parent topic and age band.
Why it matters
Parents want to understand how children learn with AI—not only what answer they repeat. Before You Believe It creates an approachable weekend moment for that conversation.
